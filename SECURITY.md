# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Either submit a report to us here on GitHub or send us a message as a human: **helloworld at panelparty.net**

**Rules:**
- Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until at least one week after our patch has been released
- We will not respond to AI/LLM-generated reports
- We will not respond to unreasonable deadlines or threats (60-90s is a perfectly reasonable fix timeline)
- We will not read reports on your security platform
